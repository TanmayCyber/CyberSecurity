# About Me

Hello everyone! My name is **Tanmay Garg**, and I am a **16-year-old** from **India**.

This repository documents my cybersecurity learning journey—the challenges I've faced while learning, my mindset during those challenges, and what I ultimately learned from them.

It also contains all of my cybersecurity notes, which are written entirely by me. My goal is to build a **strong foundation**, **track my progress**, and hopefully help other beginners who are starting their own cybersecurity journey by letting them know **what not to do**.

# Before This Repository

Before creating this repository, I had been learning cybersecurity for about a week. I started on **4 July 2026**, and by the time i created this respitory, I had learned the bare minimum Linux fundamentals, such as what Linux is, what the Linux kernel is, and a few basic file handling commands.

# Why CyberSecurity

Honestly speaking, I was interested in technology from when I was in grade **10th**, at that time I wanted to become a **software/web developer**, then AI came and slowly the demand for software and web developers diminished. So at that time I started researching about roles in the tech industry, then I came across Ethical Hacking, a job/role that piqued my interest and made me research more about it. For a year, whenever anyone asked me what was my dream job or something I would say Ethical Hacker. I even contacted our school's counselor to get more information about what I should do and how I should start learning, but he told me that everything related to hacking is illegal and didn't tell me anything else. All of my classmates made fun of me for even asking about a job or course from the counselor. I also knew before even asking that he must not know anything about Ethical Hacking, and his statement that "everything related to hacking is illegal" made it clear.

After doing some more research about CyberSecurity, I got confused about which role I should choose, so I chose to delay that decision till I knew the basics of CyberSecurity, so I could choose which one I'm truly interested in after doing it myself.

At last, the AI which forced me to change my goal from Developer to a role in Cybersecurity helped me and told me where I should start.

I am thankful to AI, because it diverted me towards CyberSecurity, as now I'm more interested in CyberSecurity than I was in becoming a Developer
