# Why CyberSecurity?

Honestly speaking, I was interested in technology from when I was in 10th grade, at that time I wanted to become a Software/Web Developer. But with the rise of AI, the demand for Software/Web Developers decreased. I began researching roles within the tech industry and came across Ethical Hacking. This role interested me so I did some more research. For a year I would tell anyone who asked me what my dream job was that I wanted to be an Ethical Hacker. I even spoke to our counsellor about what I should do and how to begin learning. He said that everything related to hacking is illegal. My whole class made fun of me for even asking. I also knew he wouldn't know anything about Ethical Hacking and his comment about everything related to hacking being illegal confirmed this.

After doing some more research into CyberSecurity I became confused about what role I wanted to go into. So I decided to put this on hold until I knew the basics of CyberSecurity so I could choose which role I was really interested in.

Ultimately, the AI that forced me to switch from wanting to be a Developer has helped me. It told me where to start.

Thank you to AI for making me switch to CyberSecurity. I am much more interested in CyberSecurity now than becoming a Developer.
